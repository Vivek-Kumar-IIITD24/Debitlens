1. Open **DebitLens** (`http://localhost:3000`). Point out the live header badges: **`LIVE_NEO4J (AuraDB)`** and **`LIVE_SARVAM (sarvam-105b)`**.
2. In **Overview Dashboard**, highlight that DebitLens analysed 45 synthetic statement rows across 3 bank/card accounts, achieving **100% precision and 100% recall** against `ground-truth.json` while keeping 3 processor-only Razorpay/Cashfree transactions (`TXN-9501`, `TXN-9502`, `TXN-9503`) strictly isolated (`UNRESOLVED`).

## Minute 0:45 – 1:45 | Three-Way Reconciliation & Section 22 StreamBox Judge Demo
1. Click **3-Way Reconciliation** in the sidebar.
2. Select **StreamBox Premium 4K (`CMT-STREAMBOX`)**. Initially, all 3 columns match:
   - **Column 1 (Documented Service Terms `DOC-STREAMBOX-01`):** `₹499` (`49900 paise`) monthly invoice
   - **Column 2 (Actual Statement Debit):** `₹499` (`49900 paise`)
   - **Column 3 (Payment Mandate `UMN-STREAMBOX-9821@okaxis`):** `₹999` (`99900 paise`) UPI AutoPay ceiling → **`MATCHED`**.
3. Click the **"Run Section 22 Judge Demo (₹499 Invoice vs ₹799 Debit)"** button in the top banner.
4. Watch the live 3-way reconciliation update immediately and persist to Neo4j AuraDB:
   - **Expected Service Invoice:** `₹499`
   - **Actual Statement Debit (`TXN-1005`):** `₹799`
   - **UPI AutoPay Mandate Ceiling:** `₹999`
   - **Outcome:** **`AMOUNT_DISCREPANCY` (`+₹300` / `+60.12%`)** with `withinMandateLimit: true`. DebitLens explains *why* the debit passed automatically without a new OTP (₹799 ≤ ₹999 mandate cap) and explicitly notes that it does **not** claim fraud.
